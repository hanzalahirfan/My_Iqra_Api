      A Letter From Your Bhai

      Dear Iqra Api,

      Happy Birthday.

      I don't know if a website can really explain what someone means to you, but I wanted to try.

      Almost 14 months ago, when I joined The Educators, I never imagined I'd meet someone there who would become like an elder sister to me. At first you were just someone I worked with. Then, slowly, things changed. You guided me when I needed guidance. You shared your experience. You inspired me with the way you carried your work and your responsibilities.

      And somewhere between all those ordinary days, jokes, problems and little memories, something beautiful happened. We became Bhai-Bhehen.

      You may not realize how much I admire you. Your consistency. Your maturity. The way you handle responsibility. How much you do for yourself and your family, for your sisters. You keep going. You guide people. And somehow you still stay kind, cute, innocent, and yourself. That combination is rare.

      I'm no longer at The Educators, but I still remember those days. There are many things I miss about that place, but the person I miss most is my Api. Not because we worked together. Because you became family.

      You made difficult days feel lighter. You gave me the feeling that there was someone I could look up to. I will always be grateful for that.

      On your birthday I don't just want to say Happy Birthday. I want to pray for you.

      May Allah keep you happy. May He protect your heart from unnecessary pain and make every difficult thing easier. May He open doors you never expected, and bless your work, your family and your future. May He give you peace where you need peace, strength where you need strength, and happiness where you deserve it.

      And when the right time comes, may Allah bless you with a husband who is caring, respectful, understanding, supportive, and perfectly suited for you. Don't rush what Allah has written for you. Trust His timing. It is always better than ours.

      So keep going. Keep believing. You are already giving 100% to yourself and your family, and I hope you never underestimate that. I know some days are heavier than others. But remember: you have already survived days you once thought were impossible. You will get through the next ones too, InshaAllah.

      I hope one day you realize that all the effort you are putting in today was building the life you were praying for.

      Thank you for being my Api. Thank you for guiding me, inspiring me, and being there. And thank you for becoming family without needing a blood relationship to define it.

      Happy Birthday, Iqra Api. May Allah bless you with a long, beautiful, peaceful and deeply happy life.

      Your Bhai ❤️
